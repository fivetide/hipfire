### Engine loop: Qwen4 DFlash2 window lanes

- **Concurrent Qwen4 DFlash2 requests verify their windows together on the engine loop.** A single-card Qwen4 load with the DFlash2 drafter now stages AR lanes that also verify external drafts (`Qwen4Bundle::stage_lanes_external`, one draft block per lane window). A greedy DFlash2 request parks into a lane at a window boundary with its draft context (`Dflash2Speculator::take_lane`): each lane drafts on its own context, then one lane step verifies the drafts of every lane (a `Copy`-source window with greedy accept and GDN row rollback) and captures the DFlash2 feature rows of the kept rows (`LaneOutputs::layer_capture`). A lane record keeps its draft context, so a follow-up turn continues live. Sampled or penalized DFlash2 requests stay on the resident state.
